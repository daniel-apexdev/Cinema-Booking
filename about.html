Our Journey

From One Screen to a Nation

We started with a single idea: that great cinema deserves a great venue — and that Ghana deserves both.

When we opened our first cinema at Accra Mall in 2019, we wanted to create something more than a place to watch movies. We wanted a landmark — a destination where the experience begins the moment you walk through the doors.

Today, Savannah Cinemas operates four premium venues across Ghana, from the bustling capital to the coastal city of Sekondi-Takoradi. We've invested in IMAX, Dolby Atmos, and luxury VIP seating so Ghanaian audiences can experience films the way they were meant to be seen.

But our real story isn't about screens or seats. It's about the moments we've shared — first dates, family outings, opening nights, award ceremonies, and quiet afternoons lost in a great film. That's the story we keep writing.

Milestones

A Few Moments That Shaped Us

2019
The First Curtain Rises
Savannah Cinemas opens at Accra Mall with 8 screens — the first IMAX cinema in Ghana.
2021
Expanding North
Kumasi City Cinema opens with 6 screens and the second-largest IMAX in West Africa.
2022
Coastal Arrival
Tema Shopping Cinema brings premium cinema to the coast with Dolby Atmos and VIP seating.
2023
The West Coast Chapter
Sekondi Cinema opens its doors — our first venue in the Western Region.
2026
The Next Chapter
Launching our Entertainment Intelligence Platform — a data-driven approach to cinema that puts audience needs first.
