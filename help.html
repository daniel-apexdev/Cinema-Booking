Booking & Tickets

Booking online takes less than a minute:

  • Browse Now Showing and pick your film
  • Select a cinema, date, and showtime
  • Choose your seats on the interactive map
  • Enter your details and confirm

Your ticket is sent instantly to your email with a QR code.

Payment

We accept:

  • Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard)
  • Mobile Money (MTN, Vodafone, AirtelTigo)
  • Cash at Box Office
  • Loyalty Points

Yes. All transactions are encrypted with 256-bit SSL and processed through PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. We never store your card details on our servers.

A small booking fee of ₵2.00 per ticket applies to online bookings. Cash purchases at the box office have no booking fee.

Refunds & Changes

Refunds are available up to 24 hours before showtime. After that, we can offer a ticket exchange for a future showtime of the same film.

Contact refunds@savannahcinemas.com to request a refund.

Yes, subject to availability. Log into your account, go to My Bookings, and select Change Showtime. Exchanges are free up to 4 hours before showtime.

If a screening is cancelled by us for any reason, you'll automatically receive a full refund within 3–5 business days, plus a complimentary ticket for a future film.

Account & Loyalty

Click Sign In at the top right, then select Create Account. It's free and takes 30 seconds. You'll earn 100 welcome points immediately.

You earn 1 point per ₵1 spent. Every 10 points = ₵1 discount. Silver members earn 1.25x, Gold 1.5x, and Platinum 2x.

See Offers for details on tiers and rewards.

On the login page, click Forgot Password. We'll send a reset link to your registered email address within a minute.
